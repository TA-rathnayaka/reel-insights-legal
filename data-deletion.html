Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 24 November 2026

"Reel Insights" does not collect personal data from Facebook users. It only reads statistics for the Page "Date Night Disasters" (video plays, watch time and retention) and keeps no profile of any person.

To remove the app's access

  1. Go to Facebook, Settings & privacy, Settings, Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  2. Find "Reel Insights" and choose Remove.

To request deletion of any stored data

Open an issue at https://github.com/TA-rathnayaka/reel-insights-legal/issues with the title "Data deletion". We will confirm within 30 days.
