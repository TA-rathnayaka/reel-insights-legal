Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

"Reel Insights" is a private tool operated by Date Night Disasters (the "Page owner"). It publishes videos to the Facebook Page "Date Night Disasters" and reads that Page's own video statistics. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up or login for visitors.

What data we use

The app uses a Page access token issued to the Page owner to (1) publish videos and reels to the Page and (2) read the Page's own video insights (plays, watch time, retention). We do not collect, store or sell personal data about people who view, react to or comment on the Page.

How data is stored

Insight numbers are stored in the Page owner's private cloud storage and used only to improve the Page's own content. They are not shared with third parties.

Your choices

To ask a question or request that anything related to you be deleted, contact us by opening an issue at https://github.com/TA-rathnayaka/reel-insights-legal/issues. See also our data deletion instructions.
